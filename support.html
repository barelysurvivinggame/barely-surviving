Barely Surviving support

Ten people, one wet coast, 100 days.

Something broken or confusing? Email barelysurvivinggame@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, what you were doing and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. We read everything and usually reply within two business days.

Where's my saved run?

On your phone. The game saves every few seconds and whenever you leave the app, so you can close it any time. Tap Continue on the title screen to pick up where you left off.

How do I move a run to another phone?

Open the game's menu and tap Save a backup file, then send it somewhere you can reach from the other phone, such as your files, a cloud drive or an email to yourself. On the other phone, tap Load a backup file on the title screen and choose that file.

Does it need the internet?

No. Barely Surviving plays offline, and there's no account to make. Ads only show when you're online.

Are there ads, timers or things to buy?

The game is free and shows a short ad between days at the start and the middle of each season, never in your first session or in the middle of anything. One purchase, Remove Ads, turns them off for good. No timers, no premium currency and no loot boxes.

I got a new phone. Do I have to buy Remove Ads again?

No. Sign in to the same App Store or Google Play account, open the game and tap Restore purchase on the title screen or in the menu.

Can I turn off the sound, the tips or the extras?

Yes. Open the menu during a run. Sound, tips, the morning plan and each extra (winter curveballs, big trips and strange sightings) can be switched on or off there.

Privacy

We don't collect any personal information ourselves. Google's ad service collects some to show its ads, and you can change your choices with Privacy choices in the menu where the law gives you some. The details are in the privacy policy.
