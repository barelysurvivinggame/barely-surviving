Barely Surviving privacy policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Barely Surviving is a single-player survival game for iPhone and Android, made by Samuel Tibbetts ("we"). This page explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version: the game is free and shows a few ads from Google. To show and measure them, Google's ad service collects some information from your phone, described below. We don't collect anything ourselves: there are no accounts and no analytics, and your saved runs stay on your phone. Buy Remove Ads and the ads stop for good, along with Google's ad service.

Ads

  • A full-screen ad can play between days, at the start and the middle of each season of a run. There are none in your first session, and none during an event.
  • The ads come from Google AdMob. To show them, keep them relevant and safe, count them and stop fraud, Google's software in the app collects: your phone's IP address (used to estimate your general location, such as your country), identifiers for your device and for this app on it, the ads you see and tap, and diagnostics about how the app and the ads perform.
  • We don't ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so on iPhone you'll never see the "Allow tracking?" prompt, and Google can't use your iPhone's advertising identifier.
  • Where privacy law requires it, for example in the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before any ads are requested. You can change them any time with Privacy choices in the game's menu.
  • Ads are limited to ones Google rates suitable for teens.
  • Google's use of this information is covered by Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment, and we never see your payment details. Once you own it, the game shows no ads and doesn't use Google's ad service. The game asks the store whether your account owns Remove Ads, so Restore purchase brings it back on a new phone.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your saved run, a short record of your past runs and your settings (such as sound, tips and the morning plan) are stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. They don't leave your phone unless you send them somewhere yourself.
  • If you tap Save a backup file, your phone's share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes, such as your files, a cloud drive or an email to yourself. The file holds only your game: the run, your survivor and your settings. Once you've sent it, the service you picked looks after it.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it saved on your phone.

What we receive

Nothing about you from the game itself. Google's AdMob reports show us totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned, not who saw them. If you've chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may pass us crash reports, which describe what went wrong in the app and don't identify you.

Children

Barely Surviving isn't made for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, for example because a new feature needs your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the game.
