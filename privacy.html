Barely Surviving privacy policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Barely Surviving is a single-player survival game for iPhone and Android, made by Samuel Tibbetts ("we"). This page explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version: we don't collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it plays without an internet connection.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your saved run, a short record of your past runs and your settings (such as sound, tips and the morning plan) are stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. They don't leave your phone unless you send them somewhere yourself.
  • If you tap Save a backup file, your phone's share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes, such as your files, a cloud drive or an email to yourself. The file holds only your game: the run, your survivor and your settings. Once you've sent it, the service you picked looks after it.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it saved on your phone.

What we receive

Nothing from the game itself. If you've chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may pass us crash reports. These describe what went wrong in the app, don't identify you, and are covered by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

Children

Barely Surviving isn't made for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, for example because a new feature needs your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the game.
