Barely Evolved privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Barely Evolved is a single-player evolution game for iPhone, made by Samuel Tibbetts ("we"). This page explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version: the game is free and shows a few ads from Google. To show and measure them, Google's ad service collects some information from your phone, described below. We don't collect anything ourselves: there are no accounts and no analytics, and your runs, your Book of Life and your records stay on your phone.

Ads

  • A full-screen ad can play between ages, as one age of Earth's history gives way to the next. There's at most one every 10 minutes, none in your first session and none in the middle of anything, and none in the daily planet.
  • The ads come from Google AdMob. To show them, keep them relevant and safe, count them and stop fraud, Google's software in the app collects: your phone's IP address (used to estimate your general location, such as your country), identifiers for your device and for this app on it, the ads you see and tap, and diagnostics about how the app and the ads perform.
  • We don't ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so you'll never see the "Allow tracking?" prompt, and Google can't use your iPhone's advertising identifier.
  • Where privacy law requires it, for example in the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before any ads are requested. You can change them any time with Privacy choices in the game's menu.
  • Ads are limited to ones Google rates suitable for teens.
  • Google's use of this information is covered by Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your run in progress, your daily planet, your Book of Life (every species you've met), your best scores, stars and lifetime totals, a short record of your past runs and your settings (such as sound, music, vibration and tips) are stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. They don't leave your phone.
  • If you tap Share this run, your phone's share sheet opens with a picture of the run (your species, how it went, its stars and points), and you choose where it goes. Once you've sent it, the service you picked looks after it.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it saved on your phone.

What we receive

Nothing about you from the game itself. Google's AdMob reports show us totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned, not who saw them. If you've chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple may pass us crash reports, which describe what went wrong in the app and don't identify you.

Children

Barely Evolved isn't made for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, for example because a new feature needs your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the game.
