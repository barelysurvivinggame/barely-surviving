Barely Evolved support

252 million years. One species. Yours.

Something broken or confusing? Email barelysurvivinggame@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, what you were doing and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. We read everything and usually reply within two business days.

Where's my run?

On your phone. The game saves as you play, at every age turn and whenever you leave the app, so you can close it any time. Tap Continue on the title screen to pick up where you left off. The daily planet keeps its own save, so it never touches your run.

Does it need the internet?

No. Barely Evolved plays offline, and there's no account to make. Ads only show when you're online.

Are there ads, timers or things to buy?

The game is free and shows a short ad between ages, now and then: at most one every 10 minutes, never in your first session and never in the middle of anything. No timers, no premium currency and no loot boxes.

What's the daily planet?

One planet a day, the same for everyone on that date: a set species in the first age of one of the five eras, with one twist, such as a long winter or a supervolcano. It's one age long. Your best score for each day stays on your phone.

Can I turn off the sound, the music, the vibration or the tips?

Yes. Open the menu during a run. Sound, the music's volume, vibration and tips are all there.

Privacy

We don't collect any personal information ourselves. Google's ad service collects some to show its ads, and you can change your choices with Privacy choices in the menu where the law gives you some. The details are in the privacy policy.
